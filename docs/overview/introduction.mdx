---
title: "Kilix Voicebox"
description: "A local studio over reusable Kilix speech providers"
---

Kilix Voicebox is a desktop product for managing voice profiles, consent-bound
speech generation, transcription requests, history, and multi-track stories.

Its defining boundary is provider-first: Voicebox owns product data but no
speech runtime. Synthesis and transcription execute only in independent,
same-UID Kilix providers reached through fixed private Unix sockets. This keeps
model installation, licence handling, resource profiles, and inference out of
the application process.

The candidate is local-only and exposes no LAN or remote-server mode.
