---
title: "Recording and transcription"
description: "Local capture with an independent transcription provider"
---

Recordings remain product-owned local files. A transcription request stages one
bounded private copy, passes one descriptor to kilix-transcribe, and removes the
staged copy after the provider returns or fails.

Voicebox does not contain or select an ASR engine. Provider capabilities and
installed models are reported by the provider inventory. The candidate keeps
text, JSON, WebVTT, and SRT as protocol result formats while selecting 0 of 4
engine routes.
